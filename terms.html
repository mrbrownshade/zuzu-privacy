3. What Zuzu is, and what it is not

Zuzu is a record-keeping tool for shared expenses. It lets you and the people in your groups record who paid for what, works out who owes whom, and helps you mark those balances as settled.

Zuzu is not a payment service, a wallet, a bank or a lender.

  • Zuzu never holds, moves, collects or transfers money.
  • The "Pay via UPI" button only opens the UPI app already installed on your phone (for example GPay, PhonePe or Paytm) with the amount and payee pre-filled. The payment itself, and any dispute about it, is entirely between you, the other person and your UPI app or bank. Zuzu has no visibility of whether a payment succeeded.
  • Balances shown in Zuzu are calculated from what you and other group members type in. They are records, not debts recognised by Zuzu. Zuzu does not verify any expense, cannot enforce a settlement and is not a party to any arrangement between members.
  • Nothing in the App is financial, tax or legal advice.

4. Groups and shared data

  • When you join a group, the other members of that group can see your name, your profile photo, the expenses you add and your balance within that group. This is how the App works; do not join a group with people you do not want to share this with.
  • Anyone who has a group's invite link or QR code can join that group. Share them only with people you intend to add. A group's admin can remove members.
  • An expense can be edited or excluded by the person who created it. Settled payments can be disputed by the receiver. These records stay visible to the group as a history.
  • When you leave a group or delete your account, expenses you were part of are kept so that the other members' balances still add up, but your name is replaced with "Deleted user" and your personal data is removed as described in the Privacy Policy.
